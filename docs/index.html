Method

Two rules this whole system runs on

They look strict. They are the difference between learning something and feeling like you did.

Attempt before you ask

Research shows unguarded AI tutoring produces better practice scores but worse unaided exam performance — a study of GPT-4 in a real course found a 17% drop once the tutor was removed. So the AI never hands me the answer. It gives one hint, then asks a harder question. I write my attempt first; the AI reacts to what I wrote.

A weekly closed-book, no-AI test

Every Sunday: 45 minutes, no AI, no notes, no browser. Whatever I can reproduce is what I actually know. Retrieval practice beats re-reading by a large margin, and the score goes on this site — including the bad weeks.
